Permissions

The app needs these to track your trips reliably, even when the screen is off.

Location and notifications
Tap Allow, then choose "Allow" on each question.
Location: "Allow all the time"
Opens settings. Tap Permissions → Location → Allow all the time.
Battery: "Unrestricted"
Android stops apps it thinks nobody is using, and a stopped app cannot report a trip. Answer Allow once and this one keeps working with the screen locked.
Driver camera
Opens the forward lens on your own phone: while a trip is running it watches automatically if your fleet turns driver alerts on, and at any other time the video button on the map starts and stops it. Either way the phone looks at your face for tiredness, distraction or a phone in your hand and warns you. The judgement happens on this phone; only the warnings themselves are filed, and nothing goes anywhere without a signal.
Alerts with the screen off
Keep Battery "Unrestricted" and notifications allowed: the buzzer and the voice are then produced by Android itself, so both work with the screen locked or another app open. Test them with the bell button once the trip has started. The speaker button at the top of the map silences every sound without losing the on-screen warning.
